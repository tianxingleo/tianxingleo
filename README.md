## Hi, I'm Leo

I'm a second-year software undergraduate at **Dalian University of Technology**, originally from Guangzhou.

I enjoy building things that people can actually use — from a small web idea shared by thousands of people to 3D projects that make research concepts tangible. My current interests are **3D reconstruction, implicit geometry, AI systems and creative software**. I also like writing down how things work (and why they sometimes don't).

## A few things about me

- **Open source:** I built [ACGTI](https://github.com/tianxingleo/ACGTI), an ACG personality quiz that grew to **1k+ GitHub stars**.
- **Competitions:** Team lead for a **National First Prize** in the 19th National University Student Software Innovation Competition.
- **Research:** Exploring sparse-view reconstruction, neural implicit surfaces and geometry processing.
- **Community:** Helping run the **Tencent Innovation Club at DUT**, where we introduce students to real projects and open-source development.
- **Beyond the keyboard:** Badminton, distance running, guitar and travel.
